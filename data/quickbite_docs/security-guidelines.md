# Security Guidelines

## Passwords
Passwords must be changed every 90 days and must have at least 12 characters.

## Lock your laptop
Always lock your laptop when you leave your desk, even for a few minutes. Press Windows + L on Windows or Control + Command + Q on a Mac.

## Data handling
Customer order data is retained for 5 years, so never copy it to personal devices.
