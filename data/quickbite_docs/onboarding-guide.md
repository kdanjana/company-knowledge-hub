# New Joiner Onboarding Guide

## Your first week
You will meet your buddy, set up your laptop and complete the security training.

## Working from home
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week.

## Core hours
Our core hours are 11 AM to 4 PM IST, so try to keep meetings inside this window.

## Support tools
All customer tickets are handled in Zendesk. Ask your manager for a Zendesk login.

## Passwords
Passwords must be changed every 60 days.

## Lock your laptop
Always lock your laptop when you leave your desk, even for a few minutes. Press Windows + L on Windows or Control + Command + Q on a Mac.
