# Expense Policy

## Meals during travel
Meal expenses during business travel are reimbursed up to ₹500 per day.

## Submission deadline
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.

## Receipts
Attach a clear photo of every receipt to your claim. Claims without receipts will be sent back to you.

## Payment
Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary.

## Alcohol
Alcohol is never reimbursed.

### Client dinners exception
For client dinners approved in advance by a director, one drink per person may be reimbursed.
