# Refund Policy

## India

### Refund window
Customers in India can request a refund within 7 days of delivery.

### Processing time
Approved refunds are processed within 10 business days.

## Eligibility
Refunds apply to missing items, wrong items and orders that never arrived. Customers must share the order ID and a short description of the problem.
