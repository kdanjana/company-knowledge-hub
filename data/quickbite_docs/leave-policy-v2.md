# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees get 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

## Carry forward
Employees can carry forward up to 15 days of unused annual leave to the next year.

## Applying for leave
Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 7 days in advance.

## Sick leave
Sick leave can be applied for on the same day by informing your manager before 10 AM.
