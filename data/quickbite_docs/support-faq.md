# Support FAQ

## How do I get my refund?
Refunds go back to the original payment method or to your QuickBite wallet, whichever you choose.

## How to request a refund
Open the order in the QuickBite app, tap "Help", choose "Request a refund" and describe the problem. Attach a photo if an item is damaged or wrong.

## When can I reach customer care?
Customer care is available 24x7 through chat in the app.

## Can I change my delivery address after ordering?
You can change the address only before the restaurant accepts the order.
