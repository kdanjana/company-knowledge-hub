# Expense FAQ

## How much can I spend on meals when travelling?
Meal expenses during business travel are reimbursed up to ₹800 per day.

## How long do I have to submit a claim?
Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense.

## Do I need receipts?
Attach a clear photo of every receipt to your claim. Claims without receipts will be returned to you.

## When will I get paid?
Approved expenses are paid out together with your next month's salary.
