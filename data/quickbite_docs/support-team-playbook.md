# Support Team Playbook

## Ticketing tool
All customer tickets are handled in Freshdesk. Zendesk was retired in January 2025.

## Response targets

### Chat
Chat support responds to customers within 2 minutes.

### Email
Email support responds to customers within 24 hours.

## Escalation
Escalate any food safety complaint to the duty manager immediately.
