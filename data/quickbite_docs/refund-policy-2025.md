# Refund Policy

## India

### Refund window
Customers in India can request a refund within 3 days of delivery.

### Processing time
Approved refunds are processed within 5 business days.

### Refund method
Refunds are credited only to the original payment method.

## UAE

### Refund window
Customers in the UAE can request a refund within 5 days of delivery.

## Cancellations
Orders cannot be cancelled for a refund once the restaurant has started preparing the food.

## Late preparation exception
If the restaurant has not finished preparing the food within 20 minutes of accepting the order, the customer may cancel and receive a full refund.

## How to request a refund
Open the order in the QuickBite app, tap "Help", choose "Request a refund" and describe the problem. Attach a photo if an item is damaged or wrong.
