# Delivery Service Levels

## India

### Metro cities
Orders in metro cities are delivered within 30 minutes of the restaurant accepting the order.

## UAE

### Dubai and Abu Dhabi
Orders in Dubai and Abu Dhabi are delivered within 45 minutes of the restaurant accepting the order.

## Contact
For delivery issues, restaurants and partners can call the operations desk at extension 4400.
