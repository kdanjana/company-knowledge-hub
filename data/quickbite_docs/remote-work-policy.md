# Remote Work Policy

## Work from home
Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week, with their manager's approval.

## Core hours
Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST. Please be available for meetings during this time.

## Equipment
QuickBite provides a laptop and a one-time home office allowance for remote work.
