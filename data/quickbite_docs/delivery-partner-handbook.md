# Delivery Partner Handbook

## Delivery time target
Orders in Indian metro cities should reach the customer within 40 minutes of the restaurant accepting the order.

## Leave and earnings
Delivery partners are independent contractors and do not receive paid annual leave. Earnings are paid every Monday for the previous week.

## Contact
For delivery issues, restaurants and partners can call the operations desk at extension 4400.
